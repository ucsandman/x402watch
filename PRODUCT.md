# x402watch

## Register

product. One dense ranked table is the whole product; design serves the task of picking a paid endpoint an agent can trust.

## Users and purpose

- Agent builders choosing which x402 endpoint to wire into a paying agent. They arrive with a need ("token prices", "weather", "permits"), search, and want to know in seconds: is it up, does it charge what the directory says, how fast is it.
- Sellers who listed an endpoint on the Bazaar or x402scan and want to see how it scores, and why.
- Agents themselves, through `/api/feed`, which returns the same rows as JSON.

The page has one job: rank listed paid endpoints by whether they actually work, with evidence.

## Brand personality

Assay office. Exact, unglamorous, slightly bureaucratic. Numbers are the voice. The tone says "we tested it, here is the stamp" rather than "trust us".

## Anti-references

- Crypto dashboards: near-black ground, neon green, glow, gradient text.
- Status-page sameness: white ground, green dots, rounded cards with soft shadows.
- SaaS hero template: big number, small label, three supporting stats, gradient accent.

## Design principles

1. Evidence beside every claim. A score never appears without the uptime, latency, price comparison, and probe tape that produced it.
2. Density over decoration. Thirty-two thousand rows; the table earns its space, the chrome does not.
3. One signature, everything else quiet: the punch tape of the last 30 probes.
4. Price drift is the loudest thing on the page. Declared price struck through, live price in red.
5. Sellers get a path to fix, not a verdict: the footer says exactly what moves the score.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast on the grey-green ground, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets on controls, reduced motion respected, tape strips carry an aria-label with the counts.
